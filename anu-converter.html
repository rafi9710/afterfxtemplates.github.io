English text

ANU fonts hold Telugu glyphs only — there is no A–Z in them, so this cannot be converted by any tool. In Photoshop put this on its own layer in a normal Latin font.

Your text stays on this device. The Telugu ↔ ANU conversion runs entirely in your browser — nothing is uploaded, and it works with no internet.
Card photos are different. A photo you drop here is sent to an AI service to be read. Please don't upload cards carrying details you would not want a third party to hold. Photos are not stored by this site.
